- Connect a real pricing ingestion/feed to GigEfx and test collection failures and data freshness.
- Integrate real research services with the multi-agent prototype and test their outputs and failure paths.
- If generated PDF answers are desired, configure and verify the separate RAG backend instead of treating passage search as LLM generation.

Do not present these missing integrations as completed. The visible demos are now explicit about their scope.
